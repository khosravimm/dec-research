## What public history proves — and what it does not

The repository is maintained through the GitHub account **@khosravimm**. Individual commits provide dated versions and content hashes, which can support a research priority, publication chronology and due diligence. **They are not notarization, patent filings, proof of an inventor's first conception, a complete record of every human/AI contribution, or a legal chain-of-title opinion.**

This public dossier was assembled with AI-assisted drafting and editorial control from a broader private laboratory research workspace. Contribution, human authorship, translation ownership and institutional/employment rights must be verified before any copyright assignment, exclusive licence, publication authorship or patent claim. Original third-party source links are credited in the catalogue, and source material is not copied into this repository.

## What is retained separately

Private or access-controlled evidence may include dated invention disclosures, design alternatives, experiment notebooks, original authorship/contributor records, implementation commits, dataset provenance, hash manifests, contractual rights and signed review decisions. None is proven merely by the presence of this public file.

## Public evidence controls

Version each bilingual document pair with a shared ID, same semantic scope and explicit E0/E1/E2/E3 labeling. Public results are published only with reproducibility material and rights review. Corrections create a new dated revision instead of silently rewriting a previously published claim.

For licensing/business discussion see [Publication/IP](docs/en/publication-ip.md); do not put confidential material into a public Issue.
