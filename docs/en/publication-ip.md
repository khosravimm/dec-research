# Publication, intellectual property and licensing

## Public disclosure boundary

This is an early-stage scientific research programme, maintained by GitHub user **[@khosravimm](https://github.com/khosravimm)**. The public site makes known research questions, cited prior art, reproducibility methods, planned tests, and eventually cleared results visible. **Unpublished implementations, proprietary techniques, private invention records, raw lab state and non-public datasets are not included.** Do not infer the existence or ownership of unregistered inventions or patents.

**Copyright and authorship:** Original material, where it qualifies for protection and is validly owned by its author/rightsholder, remains subject to applicable copyright. Legal authorship, ownership, employers' rights, AI-assisted text, translations, collaborators, dependencies and third-party claims need separate chain-of-title review. Git commits document historical snapshots; they do not conclusively establish authorship, exclusivity, inventorship or validity of rights.

**Patent/novelty:** Publishing a technical mechanism before filing may defeat novelty in important jurisdictions. Before posting potentially inventive algorithms, trainable latent fusion equations, implementation details, novel model topology, benchmark artifacts that disclose the mechanism, or presentations to outsiders, obtain a professional prior-art/ownership review and consider appropriate filing. Some jurisdictions have narrow grace periods; never rely on them without advice. Use NDA and scoped disclosure for pre-filing meetings where appropriate.

**Trade secrets:** Retain potentially protectable implementation details in controlled private records; public GitHub cannot keep public disclosures secret. Keep local SHA256 manifests, dated change records and separately controlled private experiment logs. Evidence must not contain credentials, personal data or unauthorized source material.

## Public rights statement

**© 2026 khosravimm — rights reserved in qualifying original material for which this rightsholder holds the rights.** There is **no repository-wide open-source, open-content, patent or commercial licence grant**. GitHub's Terms of Service permit platform viewing/forking and applicable statutory exceptions remain unaffected. Third-party links, code, excerpts and model references retain their original ownership/licences. For permissions, collaboration or commercial licensing, open a GitHub Issue with **non-confidential** information; exchange specific terms and disclosures separately.

No copyright notice overrides the applicable law, creates patent rights, transfers third-party rights or conclusively determines authorship. Public contributions are not accepted by default; an appropriate written contribution and rights agreement is required before merger of external code or datasets.

## Commercial paths to evaluate

Research collaboration, sponsored proof-of-concept, confidential technical evaluation, dedicated support/integration, proprietary licence, dual licensing, source licence, or an assignment/transfer agreement **subject to verified chain of title**. Product price and exclusivity are not declared. Commission a qualified IP lawyer to review jurisdiction, employer/contract claims, protectable mechanisms and trademark availability before substantive deal-making.
