# Mission amendment and architecture-neutral scope

> Research, design, and evaluate an intelligent model or an efficient ecosystem that can coordinate decision-making, compute allocation, memory, and diverse specializations through its core; while supporting resource-constrained execution, Persian, and technical tasks.

This is an **approved scope correction**, not a claim of realized architecture or proven technical superiority.

## Candidate architectures
1. **Unified model:** Decision, memory, specialization and compute scheduling are substantially internal to a model.
2. **Intelligent ecosystem:** Multiple specialized models/components coordinated by an explicit decision, memory and compute core.
3. **Hybrid:** Selected learned mechanisms inside models plus specialized external services/components.

## Decision discipline
- Keep all three options until comparative experiments on matched tasks and resource budgets justify a choice.
- Measure actual completed-task quality, calibration/uncertainty, memory behavior, CPU/RAM/latency, bilingual technical ability, provenance, maintainability, and system complexity.
- Local benchmark results on ready-made LLMs are evidence only for their tested tasks, not evidence to reject untested core-architecture research.
- Retain prior experiment records and negative findings; do not rewrite history or imply that E0 research is implemented.
- Where prior documents assumed a mandatory single integrated model, this charter supersedes that assumption; earlier candidate descriptions remain historical research hypotheses.
