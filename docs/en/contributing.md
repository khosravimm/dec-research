# Research collaboration and contribution procedure

We welcome **non-confidential** research proposals, citations, replication suggestions, negative findings and requests to collaborate. Use Issues for public discussions. **Unsolicited code, datasets, patches or weights should not be submitted** until the project maintainer defines an explicit licence and contribution/rights agreement.

A good research proposal states: which DEC research question it addresses, precise original source URL/DOI, claimed benefit, assumptions, constraints, exact proposed baseline, evaluation protocol, expected resource measurements, threat to validity, licence and potential conflicts of interest. State whether your results are reproduced E1/E2/E3 or paper-derived E0.

## Governance

A proposed change or result will be triaged as draft, under review, accepted for a preregistered test, rejected with rationale, or deferred. Acceptance as a test candidate is **not** authorship attribution, ownership transfer, co-inventorship, grant of licence, employment or partnership.

Scientific credit should follow actual contributions and the final applicable collaboration agreement. For commercial/institutional enquiries, submit only high-level details through a public Issue. Do not disclose sensitive inventions or organisation-specific information publicly. A private dialogue and NDA may be required.
